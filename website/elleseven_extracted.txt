3 | Competive Analysis

Competiton: "outdoor cabinet with a wood look" space

HDPE solid material cabinets
(Werever, American Outdoor Cabinets, Open Air Cabinetry) are the most common "durable + low maintenance" outdoor option.
They're tough and weatherproof, but the wood grain is molded into the plastic -- it looks textured, not truly wood-realistic.
PVC/resin cabinets like NatureKast replicate a wood look and are weather-resistant, but again the finish is cast, not digitally
reproduced from real wood.
WeatherStrong is probably the closest aesthetic competitor -- weatherproof with decent wood finishes -- but it's still a surface coating,
not a precision-printed veneer built around high-fidelity grain capture.
Decorative laminate veneers (like Conestoga's DLV line) exist but are primarily indoor products and don't hold up outdoors.

Allstyle occupies a genuine gap. The market has durable-but-fake-looking, or beautiful-but-not-out-
door-ready. The combination of photorealistic wood fidelity *and* true outdoor durability *and* zero
maintenance in a cabinet door format doesn't appear to have a direct match -- especially from a
Canadian manufacturer.
