01 — Patio Cabinetry

Outdoor kitchens that look indoor.

Patio cabinetry that doesn't telegraph "outdoor." Photorealistic woodgrain through our Plein Synthetic Veneer surface, marine-grade plywood cores, and a continuous waterproof top — all engineered to take a Canadian summer, then a Canadian winter, then do it again.

  • Plein Synthetic Veneer doors — real wood character, fully waterproof

  • Sealed edges with PUR adhesive — no swelling at the seams
  • Soft-close, salt-resistant stainless hardware
  • Custom configurations for grills, sinks, fridges, and pizza ovens

Flood Risk
05 — Flood Risk Environments

For when water isn't a question of if.

Basements, lake-houses, riverfront builds, and properties on insurance watchlists. Our flood-rated builds use closed-cell cores, raised toe-kicks, removable plinths, and gasket-sealed door fronts — so a rising water-line means a wipe-down, not a rebuild.

  • Closed-cell, non-absorbent core construction
  • Raised toe-kicks with removable, drainage-friendly plinths
  • Gasket-sealed doors and stainless 316 hinges
  • Wipe-down recovery rather than tear-out replacement
  • Insurance-friendly documentation and spec packages
